Susegad UI

Konkani, from the Portuguese sossegado: unhurried, at ease.

Web pages that are beautiful and comfortable at the same time. A homestay's booking page, a festival's programme, a café's menu, a proposal for a client: each one drawn with the care of a Goan afternoon, and easy for everyone to use, on any phone.

It is free. You can ask an AI agent to build your page with it, or ask your developer.

One look, three moods

Every piece comes quiet, warm or playful. Quiet suits a bank or a clinic, warm suits a homestay, and playful suits a festival. Pick one for the whole site, and every button, form and drawing follows. Try it with the View button at the top of this page.

  1. With your AI agent

    Paste this into Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or any agent that can read a web page. Change the words in brackets.

Drawn in Goa

The pictures are drawn in code, not photographed: rain on a window in the monsoon, a paddy field through the year, the ferry crossing the river, a kolam at the threshold. They move gently, rest when nobody is looking, and hold your words.

What you can count on

  • Free and open. The code is MIT licensed. Use it for any site, including one you are paid to make.
  • Made for phones. Pages load only what they need, the drawings rest when they are off screen, and the words still read with JavaScript off.
  • Easy for everyone to use. Built to WCAG 2.2 AA. Buttons, forms and menus are tested for keyboard use and for what a screen reader announces. How we check accessibility
  • Made for India. Konkani in Devanagari and Romi, lakh and crore, the rupee, and time in IST. The Konkani is a first draft until a Goan reader has checked it.
