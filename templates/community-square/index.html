Pull up a chair

New hands are welcome. Nobody is turned away for not knowing how. Write to us at the next gathering, or read how the guild works first.

Who can join?

Anyone in or near Aldona who makes things, or wants to. Members from Siolim, Moira and Mapusa come across the river too.

What does it cost?

₹3,000 for the season, paid in rupees. Guests pay ₹500 a workshop. If the fee is a problem, write to us; a member will sponsor you quietly.

Can I just watch the first time?

Of course. Come on a Saturday, sit on the bench by the shed, and ask as many questions as you like.
