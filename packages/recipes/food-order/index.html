Order fresh pasta, in a few taps

Everything is made to order, so we ask for a day's notice. Choose what you like, say when and where, and your order is written for you as a WhatsApp message. You read it, you send it, and we reply to confirm.

How ordering works

  1. Choose. Tap + beside each dish. Prices are for one of each unit.
  2. Say when and where. A day, a time of day, delivery or pickup, and your name.
  3. Send it in WhatsApp. We write the message; you press send. Nothing is final until we reply and confirm.
