Room details

A room card for a homestay. It keeps the card's shape until the details arrive, then shows them; in warm and playful it inks them in. The rate is in rupees with Indian digit grouping, availability is a badge, and if the details can't be loaded it says so and offers to try again.

These buttons stand in for the network, so you can see every path.

Every state of the card

  • Waiting for the details
  • Loaded, two rooms left
  • Fully booked, with the rate in lakh
  • Could not load
