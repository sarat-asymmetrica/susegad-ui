Saving footer

A form that saves itself when you pause typing. The footer says what is happening: saving, saved and when, or why it could not save. When the connection drops, it shows the connection's state until it is back, then saves by itself.

This switch stands in for the world, so you can see the footer when the connection drops.

Every state of the footer

  • Before any change
  • Typing, not saved yet
  • Saving
  • Saved
  • Could not save, and offline
  • Reconnecting
  • Back online, saving again
