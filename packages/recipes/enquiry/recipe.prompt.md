# Ask the house

*An enquiry form for a small guesthouse that works without JavaScript and says plainly what happens to your words.*

## The prompt

Rebuild the Casa Exemplo "Ask the house" enquiry form from Susegad UI parts only. Next to the heading "A question, or dates we can't show yet?" and its short explanation, lay out a native form with `<sg-form>`, and one `<sg-field>` for each field: your name (required), phone or email (required; a pattern that accepts either, with a hint that the house replies on WhatsApp), where they are travelling from (an `<sg-combobox>` of cities that finds older names and other scripts: Bombay, मुंबई, Bangalore), rough dates, people (an `<sg-select>` of 1 to 6 or "More than 6"), the occasion, and anything else (a ruled textarea). Word the problems in the house's voice with field notes. When someone chooses "More than 6", answer as the house would, as a hint and not an error: "The house sleeps 6. Tell us more below and we'll see what's possible." The site is a prototype, so say plainly before Send that nothing is sent. When someone sends, answer at once with "Prototype: nothing was sent. In the live site this goes to the house on WhatsApp and email." and, in warm and playful, a "Not sent" stamp; never pretend to send. Without JavaScript, the form must still be checked by the browser and submitted: post it to a static page that keeps nothing and says so, never with GET, which would put people's details in the address bar. Let the page's register choose the look through the parts: in quiet, ruled lines and words only; in warm, the rules ink under the words and the stamp is block printed; in playful, wobbling accent ink and a stamp that presses down. Keep it complete by keyboard and screen reader: every field has its visible label and hint, a problem moves focus to the first field and its note says what is wrong, and the answer is said once in a polite status line. Use the Casa palette by default, keep every colour a token, and check it in quiet, with reduced motion, and with JavaScript off. In warm and playful, put the house's front elevation under the explanation as a still line drawing in the register's ink, harvested from the villa redesign's hero drawing: calm, never moving, hidden from assistive technology, and absent in quiet.

## Words to code

| When you say | Technique | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| from Susegad UI parts only | composition | The page links the components' CSS and imports `field.js`, `field-note.js` and `form.js`. The recipe's own code is the pieces and the answer (`recipe.js`, which wires nothing when imported), the demo page's mount (`demo.js`), and the layout (`recipe.css`). |
| a pattern that accepts either | native validation | `pattern="[^@\s]+@[^@\s]+\.[^@\s]+|\+?[0-9][0-9 ]{7,15}"`: the browser checks it with or without JavaScript. The field note words it; the `title` is the browser's hint when JavaScript is off. |
| more than six … a hint and not an error | composition | People is a native `<select>` (so it cannot be invalid). `mountGroupHint()` calls `setMessage()` on a `tone="hint"` field note when "More than 6" is chosen, and clears it otherwise; the field is never marked invalid. |
| finds older names and other scripts | combobox | Each `<option>` in the `<datalist>` carries `data-aliases` ("Bombay, मुंबई"). Without JavaScript the browser shows its own suggestions from the same list. |
| say plainly before Send that nothing is sent | said before and after | A note above the button says it, and the answer says it again. The browser check confirms no request leaves the page when JavaScript is on. |
| answer at once … never pretend to send | no pretend work | `answer()` resolves immediately, so the form goes straight to "sent". No "sending" state, because no work happens. |
| post it to a static page that keeps nothing … never with GET | forms | `method="post"` to `sent.html`, which says the enquiry was not received. The check submits with JavaScript off and reads the request body. |
| Let the page's register choose the look | registers | The recipe draws nothing of its own but the house (below). Field, Field note, Form and Stamp each read `data-register`, so one attribute on the page changes the whole form. |
| the house's front elevation … a still line drawing | harvest, CSS mask | `facade.scene.mjs` is the villa elevation's `buildScene()`, copied with engine imports and without its tone and notes. `facade.bake.mjs` writes its strokes as one SVG (16 KB): integer points in relative moves, a little seeded noise for the hand, `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke` so lines stay 0.5 to 1.5 px at any size, and one path per kind of line with its own weight and opacity. `.enquiry__facade` uses it as a `mask` over `--sg-ink` (warm) or `--sg-accent-text` (playful), so it follows the palette and theme with no JavaScript; `display: none` in quiet and in forced colours; `aria-hidden`. A unit test fails if `facade.svg` drifts from its bake. |
| the Casa palette by default | tokens | `data-palette="casa"` on the root, which `?palette=` can change. Every colour in `recipe.css` is a `--sg-*` token. |
