The venue

Mandovi Riverside Hall

Riverfront Road, Panjim (Panaji), Goa 403001

Finding it

Behind the Old Secretariat, facing the river. Walk through the yellow gate with the blue tiles; the hall’s door is the second one on your left. Look for the banner.

Open in your maps app Open in a web map

Getting there

  • By bus. Any bus to the Panjim Kadamba stand, then a ten-minute walk along the river.
  • By ferry. The Ribandar ferry lands about five minutes’ walk away.
  • By car. There is a paid car park behind the hall. On a busy day, park at the Kadamba stand and walk.

Access

Step-free entrance
Yes, through the side gate on Riverfront Road. No steps.
Lift
One lift to the first floor, big enough for a wheelchair.
Accessible toilet
Ground floor, next to the foyer.
