Tinto: words in the world

The same square and the same words, three ways. First, the words sit on a panel over the picture. Then, with words="world", they are chalked on a board outside the Taverna, and each shop carries its hours on a plaque under its sign; press one for more. Last, with movable, they sit on a pane of frosted glass you can move. Quiet keeps the words flat and exact, and so does any screen too small for the board or the glass.

On glass

The same words on a frosted pane you can move. Drag the grip in its corner, or focus it and use the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps, Home to put the words back), and the people and the rain make room. The place goes into the link, so a copy of it opens with the words where you left them. Quiet keeps the words flat, and so does any screen too small to move them on.
