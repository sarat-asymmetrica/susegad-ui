After the time theory of Hindustani music, and the Goan singers who carried it: Kesarbai Kerkar, Mogubai Kurdikar, Kishori Amonkar. Taken: the idea that a colour, like a raga, belongs to an hour. Left: the music itself; the ragas are named, never imitated.
