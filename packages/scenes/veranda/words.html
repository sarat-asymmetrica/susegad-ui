Words in the veranda

The words below sit on a pane inside the drawing. Drag the grip and push the pane back behind a pillar: the pillar hides it, and the focus follows the words. Page Up and Page Down on the grip bring it forward and send it back.

Come and sit

The lamp is on at six. There is tea, and the paddy is green.

The clay pot on the seat is where the water is kept cool. It has stood there since before the roof was retiled, and it turns, slowly, when nobody is looking, so that every side of it gets the same share of the evening light.
