An exclusive group, one item open

Where is the homestay, and how do I get there?

In Aldona, a village in the fields north of Panjim. By car or bus it is about half an hour from the city; we send directions when you book.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. It is served on the veranda from 7:30 to 10, with fruit from the trees behind the house.

Can I arrive late in the evening?

You can. Tell us the time and someone will be up; the gate is closed but never locked.

All closed

Is there parking?

Yes, two cars in the shade behind the house, and room for bicycles on the veranda.

Do you take card payments?

Yes, on arrival. Cash in rupees is welcome too.

Free-standing sections, every one its own

What is the house like?

A laterite house with a tiled roof, three rooms, a long veranda and a garden the kingfishers visit.

Are children welcome?

Very much. The garden is fenced, and there is a shelf of picture books in the sitting room.
