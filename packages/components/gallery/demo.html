Photographs, consent first. Every place a photograph goes is designed for the consent that hasn't arrived yet: a pending slot shows a drawn frame with photo corners, its caption and the count, and never the image. A granted photograph opens larger in the browser's own dialog.

The homestay and its photographs are made up for the demo; the pictures are drawings in the library's own ink and wash, of places and things, not people. Every pending slot says its consent kindly: "Their permission hasn't come yet, so these stay as frames" is the idea; the count line says the number, "2 photos, coming soon". A slot with no consent attribute shows its image, because that is the author's decision; only consent="pending" hides one. Add ?register=quiet, warm or playful to see one register on its own.
