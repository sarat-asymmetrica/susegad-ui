Banner

A line across the top of a page, for news a visitor should see once: closed for the monsoon, doors open at nine. Send it away with the cross and it stays away on this browser. The first banners follow the page's register (add ?register=warm); the last three show all three at once. Every banner here has an until date far ahead, so the demo always shows; a real one carries its own.
