Toast

A short message that never takes focus. Errors and messages with an action stay until you dismiss them; the others stay long enough to read, and wait while you point at them or move into them.

Every tone

New prices The price list was updated this morning. Saved Your booking for 12 to 15 October is saved. Almost full Only one room is left for those dates. Couldn't send Check your connection and try again.

Try it

Press Alt + Shift + T to move to the newest message, Escape to dismiss it. Focus comes back to where you were.
