What we do with your details

We ask for your name and your email address.

We use them to reserve your seat and tell you if the time changes, and for nothing else.

We keep them until 30 days after the show, then we delete them.

To ask what we hold, to correct it, or to have it deleted, write to seats@tiatr.example.

You can take back your consent at any time by writing to the same address. Taking it back will not undo anything we did before you wrote, and we will tell you if it changes what we can do for you.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, tell the Sample Tiatr Night committee at the same address.
