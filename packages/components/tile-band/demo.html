Tile band

A band of hand-painted tiles for a border or a divider: Goan azulejo, the blue-and-white tiles of the old houses and churches, and its majolica cousin in cobalt, lemon and leaf green. Painted once and kept, so nothing is drawn while the page sits still.

Majolica

Down the side of a page

The same band runs down the page as an edge: it turns every motif a quarter so the vines climb, and fits whole tiles to the height.

A divider: the hr is the separator

Each band is laid from a seed, so the same page paints the same tiles every time. Add ?register=quiet, warm or playful to see one register on its own, and ?palette=azulejo for the page in the azulejo palette. In playful, pass the pointer over a tile, or tap one, and it turns a quarter and settles.
