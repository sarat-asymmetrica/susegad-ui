## What we heard

The house needs a new roof before the monsoon, and the **veranda** is where the family spends its evenings. Three things matter most:

1. Keep the laterite walls and the *oyster-shell* windows.
2. Replace the roof with Mangalore tiles on new teak.
3. Leave room for the well and the tulsi.
