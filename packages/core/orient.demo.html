Words in the world

A text panel whose supporting plane has an orientation. The register sets the cone: quiet turns nothing, warm six degrees, playful sixteen, and reduced motion is the finished still in every register. Move the pointer over the stage to turn the words.

A plane of their own

The words sit on a plane of their own, and the plane can turn. It never turns past a readability cone, it settles with a damped slerp, and it comes to rest without jitter, so a reader is not left chasing a page that will not hold still.

The text is still the layout engine's: measured once, one span a line, still selectable and still in the accessibility tree. The turn is one composited transform on the layer, so nothing reflows and nothing moves under the cursor while you read.

The words face you.

With ?register=quiet|warm|playful and ?reduced=1. No three.js is imported here: the turn is a composited transform, with or without the depth tier.
